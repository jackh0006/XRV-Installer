XRV Installer

A private SOCKS5 tunnel that travels inside ordinary Google HTTPS. One small VPS is the exit. Your devices run a client. The tunnel key never leaves your machines.

Install in one line

Server, on Ubuntu/Debian amd64 or arm64:

sudo bash xrv-install.sh

Client, on Linux or Termux arm64:

bash xrv-client.sh

Safety

Never share client_config.json, the 64-character tunnel key, a private key, or a recovery phrase. A donation is optional and never buys support or priority. Read SECURITY.md for the threat model and limits.
